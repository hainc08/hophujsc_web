03 — Factory capability

One factory, three commitments.

01

The factory

Our workshop is in Nhu Quynh, Hung Yen, on National Highway 5, with easy transport links; we deliver across northern and central Vietnam.

  • Imported machinery and equipment
  • Our own production workshop in Hung Yen
  • Delivery across northern and central Vietnam

02

Production

A closed production process, with the line continually improved to cut waste and defects and raise output.

  • Imported virgin resin
  • Logo and colours printed to your brand identity
  • Packed to your specification

03

Quality control

Strict quality control from incoming raw material to finished goods.

  • Incoming material checks
  • Every batch inspected
  • Made to the specification agreed with you

How we work together

From request to production

A clear four-step process, from material advice to made-to-order packaging production.

  1. 01

    Receive your request

    You tell us the bag type, size, quantity and print artwork.

  2. 02

    Material advice

    We help you choose HDPE, LDPE, PP or OPP and the right specification.

  3. 03

    Confirm the specification

    We agree on size, colour, quantity and technical requirements.

  4. 04

    Produce and deliver

    We produce, check quality, pack and deliver as agreed.
